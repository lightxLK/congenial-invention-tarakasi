About this exhibition

Tarakasi: The Silver Filigree of Cuttack is a single-page digital exhibition on Cuttack Rupa Tarakasi, the silver filigree craft of Cuttack, Odisha, which received Geographical Indication (GI) recognition on 15 March 2024. It was built for the Katakia Tarakasi Centre and is presented by SS Jewellers where served from ssjewellerskataka.com.

The exhibition walks through the craft's history, the stages of making, the vocabulary of wire motifs such as the spiral, curl, creeper, jaali, circle and dot, the objects and forms the craft produces, its role as the ornament set of Odissi dance, and the silver Chandi Medha backdrops of Cuttack's Durga Puja. It closes with the challenges artisans face and the sources used.

It is an educational resource, not a shop or price list. Every factual claim carries an inline citation that links to one of six public sources, listed in full at the end of the main page. Where an official roster or figure is not available online, the exhibition says so rather than guessing.

The site is static: it has no accounts, forms, database or analytics. Photographs show real filigree work from the craft's practitioners.
